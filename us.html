02 / Learn

Explore the World of Connectivity

Fiber Optics

Fiber optics transmit information through light signals traveling along specialized fibers. This technology is widely used in telecommunications networks to carry large amounts of data.

Internet Speed

Internet speed is commonly measured in megabits per second (Mbps). It is important to understand the differences between download speed, upload speed, latency and connection stability.

Wi-Fi and Networks

Wireless networks allow devices to connect without physical cables. Distance, obstacles and interference can affect wireless performance.

Digital Security

Strong passwords, two-factor authentication and recognizing suspicious messages are important habits for safer internet use.
